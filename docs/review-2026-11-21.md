## 1. The challenge so far

**What the challenge brief asks for (analytics part).** A live landing page with two variants and a privacy policy. A real PostHog A/B test where signups are the success metric. A feedback widget and a one-question survey. A named funnel "from the channel that reached them to the welcome email they opened", where every stage is a real event. A one-screen dashboard with the signup funnel, the A/B test and traffic by channel. At the end, a decision memo read against the kit's own data. The brief says PostHog does most of the grading: an automated check of the funnel, the experiment, event hygiene and hypothesis completeness. Each hypothesis must name its number and where that number lives in PostHog.

**What exists.**
- Dashboard 2381553 "ShiftPatch launch" with 5 tiles: funnel `d1JCOgLG`, experiment 612484, channel table `9s0dwQbs`, traffic `dBS4IKQb`, link clicks `ZS0Uhz2p`.
- A 5-stage funnel of real events: Landed (action 492050) → `signup_form_started` → `signup_form_submitted` → `welcome_email_opened` → `welcome_email_link_clicked` (D-040, `docs/decisions.md:427`).
- Experiment 612484 is running, with flag `hero-framing` and a buyer-role signup metric attached.
- The owner's audit (`docs/metrics-audit.md:4503`, 15:45 UTC today) shows every tile matching its raw count, except `9s0dwQbs`, which has no raw definition.

**Overall standing.** The plumbing is sound. The funnel, the experiment and the event names would pass an automated check. The weak points are three. The channel table on the dashboard has drifted from the docs and from the project test filter. The H1 to H3 hypotheses don't name an insight ID. And the data is thin, as expected for a pre-launch test with almost no outreach run. The thin data is a memo limitation, not something to fix.

## 5. Limitations for the memo

These can't be fixed by tomorrow. State them plainly.

1. **The last funnel stage is empty.** `welcome_email_link_clicked` has 0 filtered events since launch (R1). The only post-launch click is a test person.
2. **Most of the A/B sample is the owner's testing.** 7 of 11 "time" exposures and 4 of 7 signups are on the D-045 list (R5, R6). Without them, it's 6 control vs 4 time exposures, and about 3 real signups.
3. **There is one buyer-role signup in the whole window** (a3ff6e77, control). Agent 2 noted it came from iOS about 10 minutes after the owner's test burst. Treat it as unconfirmed.
4. **Only one channel has run.** `personal_network` is the only channel with live clicks since launch (R2). Direct outreach, LinkedIn and FONL were checked only before launch, because no outreach or posting was allowed (the challenge brief). Paid is a spec only. Video traffic arrives as direct (D-047).
5. **The samples are tiny.** Every rate is directional. The experiment itself reports `not-enough-exposures`.
6. **Measurement limits** (D-039 register, `decisions.md:403`):
   - People who accept cookies are counted twice at step 1.
   - Decliners' email events can't be joined to their visit.
   - Opens can be automatic (Apple Mail, Gmail image proxy).
   - Follow-up sends aren't in PostHog (D-024).
   - Survey answers stay in Google Forms (D-054).
7. **The feedback widget is live but has 0 responses** (Agent 2, system.surveys).
