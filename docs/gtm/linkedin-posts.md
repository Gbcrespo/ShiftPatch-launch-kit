# LinkedIn organic posts: "testing in the open" (6 posts)

Status: written, not published. ShiftPatch is a pre-launch concept; nothing is built. Posting waits for the owner's go-ahead (D-027).

## Why LinkedIn

LinkedIn organic ranked #2 of 19 traction channels, scoring 19/25 (`docs/research/traction-channels.md`, channel #15). The reason is that LinkedIn lists people's job titles and organizations, and its ad targeting works by "job title, company name or industry" [S10: https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/ad-targeting]. That page covers ad targeting, not organic posts. Whether Florida nurse leaders are active on LinkedIn organically is unverified; it is a reasonable assumption, nothing more. `target-customer.md` also says LinkedIn was not checked. These six posts are a test of that assumption (hypothesis H2).

**Copy**

Some context before today's question.

A January report on Florida hospital workforce numbers, covering 132 member hospitals, shows RN vacancy falling from 21% in 2022 to 10% in 2025, and turnover from 32% to 17% (source: The Capitolist, 2026-01-23, reporting the Florida Hospital Association report).

That's good news. It also makes me wonder about my own premise.

If vacancies and turnover are down, has the short-notice gap, the shift that opens up the same day, gotten smaller where you work? Stayed the same? Or just changed shape?

Short answers are welcome.

Gabriel
