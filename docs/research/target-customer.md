# Target customer: Florida hospital nursing and staffing leaders

ShiftPatch is a pre-launch concept. Nothing is built. This profile describes who the concept would approach first (the buyer side, Florida only) and what public sources say about their problem. All sources were accessed on 2026-11-18 unless noted. Items marked "national" are not Florida-specific. Items marked "search summary" were seen only in search-result text because the page itself blocked fetching; treat those as unverified until opened in a browser.

## 1. Job titles (who owns the problem or the budget)

| Role | Why they matter | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Chief Nursing Officer / Chief Nursing Executive / VP Patient Care | Owns nursing staffing, labor budget and agency decisions at hospital or system level. | Examples verified on official pages: [Halifax Health](https://halifaxhealth.org/about-us/leadership/heidi-wright/), [Jackson Health](https://nursing.jacksonhealth.org/about-us/), [HCA Florida Osceola](https://hcafloridahealthcare.com/locations/osceola-hospital/about-us/leadership), [Baptist Health South Florida nursing leaders](https://baptisthealth.net/healthcare-professionals/for-nurses/advances-in-nursing-practice/meet-our-nursing-leaders) |

| Director / Manager of float pool, staffing office or resource pool | Hands-on owner of filling open shifts. Named individuals were not found publicly in this pass (see gaps). | CFO / finance and operations | Budget for overtime, premium pay and contract labor. Not profiled in this pass. | None gathered. |

## 2. Facility type and size

- Florida has 223 hospital records in the CMS Hospital General Information dataset (state = FL, queried 2026-11-18): [CMS dataset xubh-q36u](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/api/1/datastore/query/xubh-q36u/0?conditions[0][property]=state&conditions[0][value]=FL&limit=500). That includes acute care, critical access, children's, psychiatric, cancer, VA and DoD facilities. Acute care hospitals are the relevant core.
- Ownership mix seen in the evidence: for-profit chains (HCA Florida, UHS), nonprofit systems (Baptist Health South Florida, Orlando Health, BayCare), and public systems or districts (Jackson Health, Halifax Health, Lee Health, Sarasota Memorial).
- The FHA workforce report covered 132 member hospitals, about 48% of Florida hospital beds, for May 2024 to April 2025 (via [The Capitolist](https://thecapitolist.com/florida-hospitals-add-jobs-and-ease-nurse-shortages-but-retention-challenges-remain/), published 2026-01-23). The report excluded contract labor.

## 3. Who owns the budget

- Hospital nursing labor, overtime and premium pay sit with the CNO and CFO. Jackson Health's CEO publicly framed overtime reduction as a system-level efficiency goal worth $70-80 million a year ([Miami Today, 2025-02-26](https://www.miamitodaynews.com/?p=83181)).
- Public systems publish board materials that name contract labor as a goal. A 2024 Lee Health board deck lists "reduce contract labor spend" (search summary of [the Lee Health board PDF](https://leehealth-production-website.azurewebsites.net/getmedia/c2e0d048-b919-475b-b01a-e2082405bb9e/041824SpecialBODMeetingMaterial_ada.pdf); the PDF returned 403 on fetch).
- Staffing tools or software spend: no public Florida source found in this pass naming who signs for it. This is a gap; it needs customer interviews.
