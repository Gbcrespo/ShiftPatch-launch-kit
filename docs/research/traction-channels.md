# Traction channels for ShiftPatch (buyer side, Florida)

> **Owner decision (after this research):** the three channels below were picked as recommended (D-019). Offline events stay in Florida, and the tracked link is `/go/fonl_annual_2027` rather than `fonl_event`, after the January–June 2027 events research found no confirmed Florida buyer event in that window (D-020, `events-2027h1.md`). The other slugs are unchanged: `direct_outreach`, `linkedin`, plus the `personal_network` baseline.

Research Agent 2. Started 2026-11-18 14:35:59 UTC. All sources were accessed on 2026-11-18.

ShiftPatch is a pre-launch concept and nothing is built. This file scores ways to reach Florida hospital nursing, staffing and operations leaders, the buyer named in D-014 and D-015. It recommends three channels for the owner to choose from. It is not a decision.

## 1. Framework note

- **Framework used:** the 19 traction channels and the Bullseye framework from *Traction* by Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares (Portfolio, 2015). The publisher describes the book as teaching "the nineteen channels you can use to build a customer base" and "a three-step framework (called Bullseye)" [S1]. The channel names below come from a secondary list that matches the book's structure [S2].
- **Why this framework:** it is the published source behind the "traction categories" idea. Scoring all 19 makes sure the brief's "16" doesn't silently drop any channel.
- **16 vs 19:** the brief says 16 categories, but the published book lists 19 [S1][S2]. I could not find a source for "16", so I don't know where that number comes from. The three extra channels are scored here anyway, so nothing is lost either way.
- **How Bullseye is applied:** step 1 is to brainstorm all 19 (sections 3 and 4). Step 2 is to rank them and pick an inner circle of 3 (sections 4 and 5). Step 3 is to test cheaply and then focus (sections 6 and 7). This week's tests stay within the brief's constraints: no paid spend, no public posting, no outreach, and only 10 to 15 visitors from the owner's network.

## 2. Scoring rubric

Each criterion is scored 1 (poor) to 5 (strong). The maximum total is 25.

| Code | Criterion | 5 means | 1 means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| R | Reach of *this* buyer (Florida CNO, director of nursing, staffing or scheduling office, nurse manager, finance or ops) | Reaches named Florida buyers directly | Mostly reaches the wrong people, or nobody |
| C | Cost | Free, or the owner's time only | Needs real money (ads, booth, travel) |
| S | Time to first signal under this week's rules | Gives a usable signal this week | Can't run this week, or needs months |
| F | Fit with a pre-launch concept | Works honestly with "an idea we're testing" | Needs a live product, or invites claims that it exists |
| M | Measurability in PostHog | A `/go/<channel>` link fits naturally, so it shows up as a clean `utm_source` | Hard to attribute (offline, word of mouth) |

All scores are my judgement, based on the cited facts where those exist. None of them is a measurement.

## 6. Hypotheses

Shared notes for all three:

- **Tracked link:** `/go/<slug>` fires `channel_link_clicked` (server side, with `channel`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) and redirects with `utm_source=<slug>`. The browser then carries `utm_source` as a super property on `landing_page_viewed`, `signup_form_started` and `signup_form_submitted`.
- **Where the headline number lives:** in a Funnel insight, because the server-side `channel_link_clicked` may not share a `distinct_id` with the browser (see section 7). The funnel steps are `landing_page_viewed` → `signup_form_submitted` (filtered to `is_buyer_role = true`), broken down by `utm_source`. `channel_link_clicked` counts appear in a separate Trends insight, broken down by `channel`. Test accounts are excluded (`is_internal`), and `is_preview = true` is filtered out.
- **No significance claims:** every number is a count or a rate on a handful of people. It is directional only. The thresholds below are pass/fail guides for the owner, not statistical tests.

### H1: Sales (founder-led direct outreach)

> We believe that **sending a short, plainly worded problem-discovery message (saying it's a pre-launch idea being tested) with a `/go/direct_outreach` link to the Florida buyer prospects** will result in **buyer-role signups from that link, directionally at least 2 out of 30 contacted**, validated by **Florida CNOs, directors of nursing, staffing or scheduling coordinators, nurse managers and finance or operations leaders**, using **the count of `signup_form_submitted` where `is_buyer_role = true` and `utm_source = direct_outreach`, and that count over `landing_page_viewed` with the same `utm_source`**, because **a named person writing directly about a problem the buyer owns gets more attention than any broadcast channel, and the shortage and turnover pressure Florida hospitals report keeps short-notice shifts on these leaders' minds [S7]**.

- **PostHog number:** buyer-role signups, and the buyer-role signup rate per visitor, for `utm_source = direct_outreach`.
- **Where it lives:** the channel table `9s0dwQbs` (visitors, buyer-role signups and signups ÷ visitors per `utm_source`) and the stage funnel `d1JCOgLG` (broken down by `utm_source`). `ZS0Uhz2p` shows `channel_link_clicked` by `channel`, which counts clicks, since a reply can come without a visit. As a secondary number, `survey sent` for the same `utm_source` is the last step of `KK0a37NQ`, and shows how many buyers answered the volume question. Breaking it down by answer needs the survey response property (section 7).
- **Testable now:** only the mechanics, plus a warm proxy. The owner opens `/go/direct_outreach` on an internal device and checks in Activity / live events that `channel_link_clicked` fires and that `landing_page_viewed` carries `utm_source=direct_outreach`. The 10 to 15 network visits on `/go/personal_network` show whether anyone with a buyer role signs up from a warm one-to-one ask. It is a tiny, biased sample.
- **Launch-ready, not run:** sending messages to the 30 prospects. No outreach is sent this week, and no outreach copy is written here.
- **Channel slug:** `direct_outreach`

### H2: Existing platforms (LinkedIn organic)

> We believe that **sharing honest posts and direct messages on LinkedIn about the short-notice-shift problem, each with a `/go/linkedin` link to the pre-launch concept page** will result in **visits from LinkedIn that convert to buyer-role signups at a rate no worse than the `personal_network` baseline**, validated by **Florida nursing, staffing and ops leaders who see the post or message on LinkedIn**, using **the share of `signup_form_submitted` events with `is_buyer_role = true` among all `signup_form_submitted` events, and the visitor-to-buyer-signup rate, for `utm_source = linkedin` compared with `utm_source = personal_network`**, because **LinkedIn shows people's job titles and organizations, so the sender can choose who sees the link, and its ad platform targets by job title [S10] (Florida nurse leaders' organic LinkedIn activity is an unverified assumption)**.

- **PostHog number:** the buyer share of signups, and the buyer-signup rate per visitor, for `linkedin`.
- **Where it lives:** the channel table `9s0dwQbs`, comparing the `linkedin` row with the `personal_network` row, and the stage funnel `d1JCOgLG`. `d9eeKWIG` (signups by role, by `utm_source`) shows which buyer roles (or nurses or vendors) LinkedIn brings.
- **Testable now:** only the redirect and UTM mechanics, through an internal click on `/go/linkedin`. Posting is public and not allowed this week, and DMs to non-network contacts would be outreach.
- **Launch-ready, not run:** the link and both insights. Post and DM copy should be written later, owner-approved and pre-launch-honest.
- **Channel slug:** `linkedin`

### H3: Offline events (FONL chapters, HFMA Florida)

> We believe that **having in-person problem conversations at Florida nurse-leader and healthcare-finance meetings, and handing out a card that says ShiftPatch is a pre-launch idea being tested, with a QR code to `/go/fonl_annual_2027`** will result in **a higher buyer share of signups than any online channel, and more volume-question answers per signup**, validated by **nurse leaders at FONL chapter meetings and FONL's annual conference, and finance and ops leaders at HFMA Florida events**, using **the count of `signup_form_submitted` where `is_buyer_role = true` and `utm_source = fonl_annual_2027`, the buyer share of all signups from that source, and `survey sent` per `signup_form_submitted` for that source**, because **FONL gathers Florida nurse leaders in one place through five regions and a statewide conference [S3][S4], and someone who has already talked through the problem face to face is more likely to sign up and answer the volume question**.

- **PostHog number:** buyer-role signups and the survey-answer rate for `fonl_annual_2027`.
- **Where it lives:** the channel table `9s0dwQbs` for the buyer share of signups, and `KK0a37NQ` (visit → buyer signup → `survey sent`, by `utm_source`) for the survey-answer rate. `ZS0Uhz2p` shows `channel_link_clicked` by `channel`, which counts QR scans.
- **Testable now:** nothing with buyers. The 2026 FONL conference has already happened (November 4–6, 2026), and the next one is November 3–5, 2027 [S3]. No chapter meeting dates were verified for this week. The owner can scan a test QR code on an internal device to check the redirect.
- **Launch-ready, not run:** the slug, the QR code and the insight. The card text needs owner approval and must not claim the product exists. If HFMA Florida events are used too, give them a separate slug (`hfma_fl_event`) so the finance-buyer response can be read on its own.
- **Channel slug:** `fonl_annual_2027` (D-020; originally proposed as `fonl_event`, optionally plus `hfma_fl_event`, which was not built)

**Baseline this week:** `/go/personal_network` (D-007). It is the only link that will get real visits this week. Read it in the same Funnel insight. It shows whether the funnel records the visits correctly and gives a rough warm baseline. It is not evidence about the channels above.

## 7. Proposed tracking changes (for the owner to decide)

None of the three hypotheses needs a new event. Two clarifications would make the numbers cleaner:

1. **`distinct_id` on `channel_link_clicked`.** The tracking plan doesn't say which `distinct_id` the server-side Pages Function uses. If it doesn't match the visitor's browser id, `channel_link_clicked` can't be a step in the same funnel as the browser events, which is why the hypotheses above read it in a separate Trends insight. **Proposal:** write down the `distinct_id` behaviour in `tracking-plan.md`. Funnels keep starting at `landing_page_viewed` unless the owner decides otherwise.
2. **Survey response property.** H1 and H3 would benefit from breaking down `survey sent` by the answer to the volume question. PostHog records the answer as a property on `survey sent`, but `tracking-plan.md` lists no survey properties. **Proposal:** add the survey response property (PostHog's own name for it, to be confirmed in the PostHog project) to the tracking plan's built-ins table. Until the owner approves it, the hypotheses use only the `survey sent` count.
