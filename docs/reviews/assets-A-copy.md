# Reviewer A (Copy): pre-submission review of the ShiftPatch launch kit

**Date:** 2026-11-21 (submission 2026-11-22)

## Summary

1. Honest framing is strong almost everywhere: every asset says "concept" and "nothing is built", and no asset claims users, pilots, results, a launch date or pricing. The weak spots are small: the video's "Meet ShiftPatch", "Open shifts, filled from within" and "what's next", plus a few present-tense lines.
2. The one visible tone problem is the outreach: all 20 first messages (and most connection notes) share one skeleton and a verbatim middle sentence. A reviewer will read them as templated.
3. Posts, ads and emails read as one person writing. No exclamation marks, emoji, hype words or hashtag overruns were found. Remaining items are minor: unsourced "usually" in hero A, the pain stated as fact in hero B (owner-approved in D-010), and a survey-length mismatch between the page and the emails.

**Count: 1 major, 12 minor, 14 nit (27 findings).**

- Video: "Meet ShiftPatch", "Open shifts, filled from within", "Short-notice shifts, from your own team." and "what's next" (#17 to #20) are the lines closest to implying availability. - "Not X, but Y" constructions: two mild ones (#22, #23).

- Phoning-around as a fact: hero B (#10) only. Posts (post 6), ads, outreach, video (script line 17) and emails ask it as a question or avoid it.
